Pantry Logic — Less figuring out food. More eating it.

One shared place for your household's grocery list, the food you have on hand, and the meals you like to make. No more "what do we have?" and "what should we eat?" text threads.

Open Pantry Logic

Thanks for testing! Everything in this guide uses a practice household, so the screenshots are exactly what you'll see.

The app in five tabs

Home — a quick look at today.

Grocery — what to buy. Tick it off when you've bought it.

Stock — what you have, and where it lives (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry…).

Meals — meals your household makes, with ingredients and directions.

Calendar — pick a dinner for each day of the week.

They all connect: buy something and it lands in Stock; a meal checks Stock to see if you have its ingredients.

Getting started

One person sets up the household. Everyone else joins with a short code. Pick whichever describes you.

A. You're the first one: start the household

Lost the code? It's always in Home → the gear icon (Settings) → Invite code. Tap it to see and copy it.

B. Someone already set it up: join with the code

About that "Guest session" note. When you join with a code, you're a guest on that phone or browser. As far as I can tell in the app today, there's no button to turn a guest into an email account, so keep using the same device and avoid clearing the browser's site data. Switching phones means joining again with the code.

Signing in later

Sign out any time from Settings (bottom of the page).

Grocery list

Add things as you think of them. Tick them off at the store, and they move into your Stock automatically.

  • Ticking something off puts it in Stock. It goes to the place the app has learned for that item (otherwise Pantry), and its quantity is added to what you already have. You can move it afterwards (see Stock).
  • Changed your mind? Swipe an item to the left to remove it, or open it and choose Delete item.
  • Un-ticking an item doesn't take it back out of Stock.

Keeping track of food (Stock)

Stock is what you have right now, sorted by where it lives. Keep it rough. A few taps is plenty.

  • At the bottom of the edit sheet: Add to grocery list (running low?) and Remove from stock (all gone).
  • Quantities are free text, so "2", "500 g" or "a few" all work. The – / + buttons only appear for plain numbers.
  • The places (Fridge, Freezer, …) can be renamed or added to in Settings → Storage locations.

Meals and recipes

Save the meals your household makes, list what each one needs, and the app tells you whether you have it.

  • The Eat This button at the bottom of a meal is covered in the next section.
  • The ⋮ menu on a meal lets you rename it, change its category or delete it. The categories icon on the Meals screen manages categories.
  • You don't need directions. The Directions tab says "No steps needed? That's fine."

The "I'm Hungry" button

Can't decide? Tell the app what kind of meal you want and it deals out one idea at a time, starting with the ones you can make right now.

What it really does

  • Order: inside the category you chose, meals you can make from Stock (or that have no ingredients listed) come first in random order, then the others, also in random order. It does not learn your tastes, look at the time of day or remember what you ate lately.
  • "Have everything" means every listed ingredient is in Stock (and, where you set a count, there's at least that many). If an ingredient's quantity is text the app can't read ("a few"), the card says Check quantity instead.
  • Eat This takes stock off only for ingredients where you set a count. In the example, eggs go from 12 to 9 because that meal needs 3 eggs. Ingredients with no count show Not tracked and stay as they are. Anything short, unreadable, or not in stock is skipped and labelled. If an amount hits zero, the item is removed from Stock.
  • If a meal has no ingredients at all, Eat This just says "Enjoy …!" and changes nothing.
  • The suggestion card doesn't show directions. Open the meal from the Meals tab for those.
  • Eat This doesn't put the meal on the Calendar. Use the Calendar for that.

Planning the week (Calendar)

Optional, but handy: one dinner per day, and the app shows if you have what it needs.

Using Pantry Logic together

Everyone who joins your household sees the same grocery list, stock, meals and calendar.

  • Example: one of you adds eggs to the list while the other is at the store. They see it appear and grab them.
  • The grocery list updates live (I tested it with two devices). The Stock, Meals and Calendar screens are built to update live as well. If something ever looks out of date, switch tabs and back.
  • Things you add are labelled with your name.
  • The person who created the household is listed as owner in Settings. Everyone else just uses the app.

Everyday examples

"The milk is almost gone."

  1. Open Grocery and type Milk. Press Enter. Everyone can now see it.
  2. Optional: tap Milk and add a quantity like "2".
  3. Or from Stock: tap Milk's name, then Add to grocery list.

"I'm home and want dinner."

  1. On Home, tap I'm Hungry.
  2. Choose Meal (or Snack / Light Meal).
  3. Not feeling it? Tap Suggest Another.
  4. Happy? Tap Eat This, check the list, then Done - Update Stock.

"I'm putting groceries away."

  1. If you shopped from the list, tap each circle on Grocery as you unpack. It all goes into Stock for you.
  2. Bought extras that weren't on the list? In Stock tap Add to stock…, pick Fridge, Freezer or Pantry, and type them in.
  3. Check the Stock tab. Move anything that landed in the wrong place by tapping its name.

"What do we have? I don't want to dig through the fridge."

  1. Open the Stock tab. Everything is grouped by Fridge, Freezer and Pantry.
  2. Looking for something specific? Tap the magnifier at the top right and search.
  3. Want to know if you can make a meal? Open it on the Meals tab. Each ingredient says In stock or Missing, and Add … missing puts the gaps on your list.

Quick reference

The main screens at a glance.

Home

What it's for
A snapshot: items to buy, items in stock, tonight's and tomorrow's dinner.
Use it when
You open the app and want the quick picture, or you're hungry.
Most common action
Tap I'm Hungry.

Grocery

What it's for
The shared shopping list.
Use it when
You run out of something, or you're at the store.
Most common action
Type in Add an item…, or tap the circle to tick something off.

Stock

What it's for
What you have, by Fridge / Freezer / Deep Freeze / Pantry.
Use it when
You've put food away or used some up.
Most common action
Add to stock… or tap – / +.

Meals

What it's for
Your household's meals, with ingredients and directions.
Use it when
You're saving a new meal or checking what a meal needs.
Most common action
Open a meal and read the Ingredients and Directions tabs.

Calendar

What it's for
One planned dinner per day for the week.
Use it when
You want to plan ahead.
Most common action
Tap a day and pick a meal.

I'm Hungry

What it's for
Picking something to eat, favouring meals you can make now.
Use it when
Nobody can decide.
Most common action
Pick a category, then Suggest Another until you like one.

Settings

What it's for
Your name, the household name and invite code, members, storage places, meal categories, sign out.
Use it when
You need the invite code or want to rename a place.
Most common action
Open Invite code to share it.
